About us

A team of engineers, lifting Bangalore higher.

Founded in 2018 in Marathahalli, Bangalore, Universal Elevators has grown into one of the city's most trusted elevator dealers and installers — without losing the hands-on, customer-first ethos that started it all.

Our story

Universal Elevators was founded by a team of qualified lift engineers who believed Bangalore needed a more honest, design-led approach to elevators. Eight years on, we still operate by the same principles: hire skilled engineers, keep work in-house, and treat every project like it's our own building.

Today we serve homeowners, builders, apartment associations and businesses across Bangalore — handling everything from residential home lifts to large commercial installations and ongoing AMC service.
