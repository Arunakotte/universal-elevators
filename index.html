Turnkey Execution

How It Works

A seamless 6-step architectural journey from initial concept to certified handover and ongoing care.

01

Consultation

Understanding building usage, floor traffic, aesthetic preferences, and preliminary budget.

02

Site Assessment

On-site survey of shaft dimensions, pit depth, overhead headroom, and power connections.

03

Design & Planning

Engineering CAD approval, drive sizing, cabin material finishes, and fixed BOQ quotation.

04

Installation

Precision mechanical guide rail erection, machine placement, car assembly, and wiring.

05

Testing & Handover

Dynamic load testing, safety gear validation, BIS compliance sign-off, and customer training.

06

After-Sales Care

Scheduled preventive AMC audits and 24/7 round-the-clock emergency assistance.
