FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does an elevator installation cost in Bangalore?
Costs vary by type and capacity. Home elevators typically start from ₹6 lakhs, passenger lifts for apartments from ₹10 lakhs, and goods lifts from ₹8 lakhs. After a free site visit we provide a fixed-price quote with no hidden charges.
How long does an installation take?
A residential home lift takes 7–15 days on site after manufacturing. Passenger lifts for apartments typically take 4–8 weeks. Manufacturing lead times are usually 6–10 weeks depending on cabin specification.
Do you offer AMC (Annual Maintenance Contracts)?
Yes. Our AMCs are tailored to lift type and usage, with options for comprehensive or non-comprehensive cover. All contracts include 24/7 emergency call-out across Bangalore.
Are your installations compliant with Indian standards?
Yes — every installation complies with IS 14665 (Indian Standard for electric traction lifts) and applicable BIS norms, with full certification provided on handover.
